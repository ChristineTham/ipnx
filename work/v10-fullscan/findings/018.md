## binary files

**None.** All 145 are ASCII hex text. Proof they carry no text:
`tr -d '0-9a-fA-FxX, \t\n' < f | wc -c` returns **0 for every one of the 145** — not one file
names a path, a person, a program or a comment.

Format: 48 lines of `0xXXXX,0xXXXX,0xXXXX,` — the single exception `tanuki`, 144 lines of one
word each. Sizes are only three values, and the variation is punctuation, not content:
- **1056 bytes (64 files)** — `0x0000,0x0000,0x0000,` , no spaces.
- **1152 bytes (75 files)** — `0x0000, 0x0000, 0x0000,` , one space after each comma. (`tanuki`
  reaches 1152 by a different route: 144 lines of 8 bytes.)
- **1200 bytes (6 files)** — as 1152 plus one trailing space per line: `sean.old`, `stan`,
  `terry`, `texhax`, `uml`, `zeydy` (verified by `od -c` on line 1: `... , \n` preceded by a
  space).

Hex case is mixed across the set — lowercase and uppercase files sit side by side, and
`turkey.recline`/`turkey.updown` are the same image in the two cases. Nothing reads case:
`face.c:97-107`'s `HexToDec` accepts `'A'-'F'` and `'a'-'f'` alike.
