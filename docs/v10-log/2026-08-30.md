# 2026-08-30 — the build learns the rest of the distribution

The mandate: the build genuinely reproduces a complete distribution from
source.  The measure: the machine itself — `ls /bin /usr/bin /usr/lib /etc
/usr/games` on a clone, diffed against what the driver builds and installs.
The diff started at 31 unmentioned binaries out of 352 and ended at a handful
of recorded parks.

## Games

`/usr/src/games` was outside the build entirely — srctotape lists it, Admin's
install lists cannot name a single game's destination.  The driver now builds
and installs thirty-two: eleven directory games (atc with its `lib/atc` data,
sail's three binaries and shipslog, rogue and mille through `make` since their
mkfiles still carry `.SUFFIXES`, adv with `lib/cave`, pacman installed as the
tape's install says, des with the encrypt/decrypt link, word_clout's script
and `lib` binary) and twenty-one single-file games, worm and hanoi carrying
their curses links.  `rnd` turned out to be a program, not a subroutine —
read before wiring.

W15's first "success" was a lie worth recording: 31 games installed, all
stale tape debris the install rules had copied; W16's clean swept it and W17
met the real compiles.  What they taught, stratum by stratum, all in
build/patch with reasons:

- gets stays deleted (td, 88.11.07); hangman, imp, rogue, mille, sail and
  netnews's five real sites got the fgets idiom.  The other apparent sites
  were already fgets or hfgets — `grep -c 'gets('` counts those too.
- trek is pre-K&R: 124 archaic `=+`/`=-`/`=/` assignments (the second sed
  died of its own `|` delimiter against the `=|` operator — comma delimiters),
  then ten bare-brace initializers, and beneath those warp.c assigns pointers
  to ints.  Parked at that stratum; the tape's own build never compiled it.
- pacman just never included `<sys/filio.h>` for FIONREAD; BSD's sgtty.h
  used to drag it in.
- sail's `vwait(&status, flags, 0)` is wait3 by another name.

## The command families

The 42 unbuilt cmd directories divided by evidence.  Wired and proven: asd
(five programs; its `lorder | tsort` retired — lorder is on no tape and no
machine, and ranlib makes order moot), chuck, error (`-O`, since `-Od2`
overflows word displacements — des's encrypt the same).  Parked with counted
reasons: pico (`/usr/local/lib/libfb.a` nowhere), poly (`poly.h` nowhere),
vsw (its mkfile opens CC=CC — C++), and charge/dumpcatch/iostat, which the
driver's own NOT BUILT block had already adjudicated with better evidence
than mine.  agh.c was recorded there as unreachable — norman-only, never
extracted — and now rides build/mkfiles, so its entry was corrected rather
than obeyed.  dump needed V10's typed fstab (`fs_ftype`, and `fs_freq`
shimmed to `fs_passno`); server needed libdk built first; weather reads with
bopen/bread, the pre-fio buffered IO no tape carries — parked.

Bigger trees: nupas — cmd/nupas/v10 is the tape's own V10 configuration of
the mailer, norman ships its config.c and sys.h — builds whole once the
converter's `; || :` mangling of make's leading dash was mended.  netnews
builds inews, readnews and checknews after four pathinit rules lost their
`-D` dashes, one rule the tape itself never quoted right, and
`-Dindex=strchr`.  ether installs libether and two of three configs
(ipconfig's `tcp_addr()` is a lost Bell function).  dk installs libdk and
its commands — the sys/ioctl.h story ran through it all: that header is on
no tape, and its consumers each wanted a different survivor — filio for the
line-discipline pushes, dkio for KIOC*/DIOC*, enio and inio for the ethernet
and IP sets, nttyio for tchars — so the includes were redirected per file
rather than the header invented.  dcon alone goes deeper, into BSD sgttyb
members the streams tty reshaped, and parks.  The index/rindex shim closed
the last dk and server links.

The census also un-invented most of itself: csh, `[`, the uucp fifteen, the
pascal family, monk's commands, compact's siblings and compress's links were
already in the driver — the image predates the rows, which is what the
machine-census diff is for.

## Converged at W30

Fresh clone, end to end, clean halt.  Census: cref-ign, struct, worm —
tape-vintage rot, each recorded — and backup.old on the rounds where the
no-swap fork peak bites it (its own retry succeeds).  Everything else
builds and installs: 32 games, all seven dk commands (ndcon came back once
filio joined types ahead of dk.h — its `undefined case label' cascade was
the include, not rot), the mailer, the news system, ether's library and two
configs, server, dump, asd's five, and the rest of the census.  The tar of
/usr is clean: 4 `.a`, the four spared names, and zero case duplicates.
libnm stays unwired until the project's strtod stops looping on
long-mantissa D-float constants — the 0d-constant block in the driver had
already named the fault; dcon parks in the streams-tty gap, trek at its
pointer-punning stratum, and pico, poly, vsw, weather, ipconfig, charge,
dumpcatch and iostat each carry their counted reason in the driver.

## ipnxbuild installs again, and the tar meets the repository

The build/install split was this window's invention, never sanctioned: the
committed original has no split — "Every target is an installed file" — so
building was installing, into the running system.  ipnxbuild now runs world's
members after the quarters, each in its own mk, and a fresh clone comes out
of `ipnxbuild` alone with sh, init, the games, the dk tools and the rest
installed.  Installing over a live system taught its own strata: the running
sh and init take mv, not cp (Text file busy); ln rows must rm first; make's
`$$i` quoting reached sh as pid+i in five more mkfiles; compress's usermem
is the generator script and my casefix row had deleted it — withdrawn,
reshipped, USERMEM swept instead.

The current tar (461M) now sits at work/clone/inx.tar and extracts on APFS
— one junk-file/Junk-directory pair survived the slash-blind dup check and
got its row.  Extracted against v10/usr: 3,790 differences in three
explained classes — repo-only tape objects the machine's clean sweeps plus
the 21 directories awaiting the keeper's rm; tar-only build residue
(including .ax$$ nests from arcfix re-extracting the build's own remade
sys/lib archives — the x-op now skips a stem directory that already
exists); and differing files that are the HISTORICAL stamps and the
on-machine patch repairs, by design.  So the measured answer to `rm -rf
v10`: the repository and the tar are different objects on purpose — the
repo keeps the tape's text and the repairs as a script; the tar is the
machine's living state.  Equivalence would mean abandoning that design,
which is the keeper's call, not a cleanup.
